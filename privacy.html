Privacy & data use

SurakshaMap is designed as a browser-only prototype for community safety reporting. This page describes what the supplied prototype code does with report information.

What you enter

  • Issue category, severity and description.
  • Optional photo.
  • Approximate latitude and longitude, either entered manually or supplied by the browser's location permission.

What is not requested

The report form does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.

Where data is stored

Reports are saved in the browser's localStorage. Tracking therefore works in the browser where the report was saved. The prototype does not include a server or database.

Location and photos

The application rounds the latitude and longitude before saving a submitted report. Photos are resized in the browser and stored as part of the browser-local report data in this prototype.

Maps and external resources

The public site loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap through Leaflet and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those external services may process requests made by your browser according to their own policies.

Important limitation

This is a hackathon prototype, not a production privacy or security system. The demo admin login is frontend-only and should not be treated as secure authentication.
